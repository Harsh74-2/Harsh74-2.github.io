Bachelor Thesis

Accelerating Spatial Universe Simulations: A Performance Analysis of Neural Network Surrogates vs. Traditional Algorithmic Models in Python

Submitted 25 Sep 2026
IU International University of Applied Sciences, Germany
  • Built per-body, variable-N surrogate pipelines in PyTorch (MLP, LSTM, graph neural networks) for 3D gravitational N-body simulation, trained at body counts N = 10, 25, 50 and 100.
  • Validated all three architectures out of distribution on real Solar-System initial conditions from NASA JPL Horizons ephemerides across 7 presets.
  • Designed the evaluation methodology: held-out test splits, unified metrics across model types, and a rollout-stability benchmark quantifying autoregressive error compounding (~5x higher error with feedback loops).
  • Published all results, animations and dashboards as a public benchmark site: harsh74-2.github.io/N-body-surrogates.
